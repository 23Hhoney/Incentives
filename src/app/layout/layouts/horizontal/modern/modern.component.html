Points On Hold

Your Points are on hold for one (or two) of the following reasons:
1) We do not have your W9 on file
2) You have not passed all the Required Monthly Training which are currently posted

W9 Submission Instructions:

To complete and submit your W-9 form, download the form below and fill out the required fields. When form is completed, please fax or email form (email subject: [Your BP#] W9) to Kohler Preferred Partners at 212-208-0926 or tritonsupport@kohlerpreferredpartners.com. You can also upload your W9 to your account by clicking “My Account” and clicking “Edit” next to “Manage Tax Information”.
To complete the W-9 form:
1) Please write your BP# (The number you use to login to Kohler Preferred Partners) at the top of the form.
2) Please check off the "individual/sole proprietor" as your federal tax classification.
3) Make sure to add your Social Security Number to the section below, Part 1 "Tax Payer Identification Number (TIN)." Please do not fill out the Employer Identification Number (EIN). This is in accordance with income tax filing procedure.
4) Please fill out your current home address and sign the form.
Please download your W9 form here: W9-Form.pdf
IMPORTANTNOTE:
You will not be able to redeem points earned on the Kohler Preferred Partners site until your completed and signed form has been submitted and processed. Please allow 2 business days for forms to be processed.
