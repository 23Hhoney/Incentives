Frequently Asked Questions

Load My Card

I can't find the email with my redemption load link. What should I do?

Please check your "Trash" and "SPAM" folders for any emails that might have been filtered there. If you still can't find the email, try searching for the subject line: Your Kohler Direct Gift Card or search for the email address: noreply@yourdigitalreward.com.

When are my points eligible for redemption?

Kohler Preferred Partner Points become eligible for redemption via the KOHLER Direct Gift Card program as soon as they're deposited in your account, you've submitted a W9 form, and you're up-to-date on all required training courses.

What are the minimum and maximum amounts I can redeem?

You can add a minimum amount of 4 points up to 12,827 points per redemption transaction, once per day.

Will my Kohler Direct Gift Card code ever expire?

Yes, the load value codes for the Kohler Direct Gift Card expire on December 31 of the following year. For example, a code purchased on July 1, 2023, will expire on December 31, 2024. However, once you activate your load value code and redeem it for a gift card, that gift card itself does not expire.

Please note that VISA Rewards card products typically do expire and expired VISA Rewards card funds cannot be reissued or replaced.

Where can I use my Kohler Direct Gift Card load code?

The Kohler Direct Gift Card can be exchanged for eGift cards from a wide variety of brands in our catalog. Most brands offer denominations from $5 to $500, with some even providing options as low as $1. You have the flexibility to apply the entire load value to one brand or distribute it among different brands.

When I click to redeem, I am directed to another website. Is this part of the process?

Yes, it is. You will be taken to a separate platform to redeem your points for the Kohler Direct Gift Card. This is where you can access a variety of eGift cards to choose from based on your load value.

I need assistance with my gift card or have lost it. What should I do?

For any balance or functionality issues with the eGift card itself, you'll need to contact the retailer directly. For redemption process issues before selecting a brand card, please get in touch with our customer support via email at rewards@kohlerpromotions.com.

How do I check the balance on my load value code?

If you've partially used a load value code, you can revisit the redemption platform to view the remaining balance and choose how to use the rest by clicking on your initial redemption load link sent to your email. This system allows you to keep track of your expenses and remaining funds.

How do I check the balance on my gift card?

Please use the card retailer's resources (which will be delivered with your card upon redemption) to manage your gift card balance.

Can I use my eGift card in person?

The method of using eGift cards in person varies with each retailer, as they may use QR codes, barcodes, or other formats. Detailed redemption instructions will be provided with your reward, ensuring you can redeem your eGift card easily - most often directly from your mobile device or by a printed copy, if preferred.

I need help with a gift card, who do I contact?

The contact details for customer service of each eGift card are provided in its Terms & Conditions. For help with the redemption process or any part of the load value experience before choosing a brand card, you can consult our customer service via email at rewards@kohlerpromotions.com.
