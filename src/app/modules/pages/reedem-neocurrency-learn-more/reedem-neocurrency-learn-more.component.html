All About KOHLER Rewards Prepaid MasterCard
Need a Card? Here's How to Get One
01 Enter the number of points you want to redeem and click Redeem

02 Click on the hyperlink that will be displayed after your transaction. This will take you to the card management site where you can input all of your requested information

03 You will then receive a temporary electronic card you can use immediately

04 Your physical KOHLER Rewards Prepaid Mastercard will arrive in 7-10 business days

Once you receive your physical card, to add more value, simply visit Kohler Preferred Partner, enter the desired amount of points, and click 'Redeem'. It's that simple!

How to Redeem Points

1. On the Kohler Preferred Partner site select 'Redeem' then 'KOHLER Rewards Card'
2. Enter the number of points you want to redeem
3. Your card is loaded with the corresponding dollar amount and ready to use almost immediately

Things to Know

Kohler Preferred Partner Points become eligible for redemption on your KOHLER Rewards Prepaid Mastercard® as soon as they’re deposited in your account, you’ve submitted a W9 form, and you’re up-to-date on all required training courses.
There is a minimum of $100 and a maximum of $10,000 per redemption transaction, which can be redeemed once per day.
Your Kohler Rewards Prepaid Mastercard will expire 3 years from the date it is generated. Upon expiration, you will be issued a new card to the address you have on file at account.mypaymentvault.com.
