All About KOHLER Direct Gift Card Program

The Kohler Direct Gift Card program offers a seamless way to transform your Kohler Preferred Partner points into a load value code that can be redeemed for a vast array of single-use eletronic gift cards available through the Kohler Direct Gift Card Catalog, which features a diverse selection from top brands and retailers.

If you prefer something tangible, opt for a single-use physical VISA reward card, valid wherever VISA is accepted.

How to Redeem Points

Step 1: Explore Your Options

Visit the Kohler Direct Gift Card Catalog by clicking here to peruse the selection of electronic gift cards, including a VISA Rewards card with a physical delivery option.

Step 2: Convert Your Points

After choosing your desired card, navigate to the redemption page to turn your points into a load value code. Simply input the amount of points you wish to use in the designated field and the dollar value of your points will be displayed.

Step 3: Redeem Your Code

Hit the 'Submit' button, then follow the onscreen link to proceed (rest assured, this link will also be sent to your email should you exit this page by accident).

Step 4: Finalize Your Selection

Use the load value code from Step 2 to complete your purchase in the catalog. Your electronic gift cards will be delivered to your email inbox almost instantly, ready to be used or gifted as you see fit.

Remember, each load value code is unique and cannot be combined with others; however, any remaining balance on a code can be used at any time before its expiration date.
