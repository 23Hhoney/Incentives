Frequently Asked Questions

Load My Card

When are my points eligible for redemption?

Kohler Preferred Partner Points become eligible for redemption on your KOHLER Rewards Prepaid Mastercard® as soon as they’re deposited in your account, you’ve submitted a W9 form, and you’re up-to-date on all required training courses.

How much can I load on my card account per redemption?

You can add a minimum amount of $100 up to $10,000 per redemption transaction, which can be redeemed once per day.

Will my card ever expire?

Yes, but not for three years. Plus, funds are automatically uploaded to the existing card, eliminating the hassle and wait time of receiving a new card to use when redeeming points.

Where can I use my Mastercard Prepaid Card?

Just about everywhere! Mastercard is accepted by most businesses and retailers all across the country!

When I click redeem, I am directed to another website. Is this ok?

Yes, this means our interface is working correctly! You'll be redirected to a separate website to register for your card and manage your account balance.

I need assistance with my reloadable gift card or lost it. What should I do?

Please call +1 (888) 633-1275.

How do I check the current balance on my debit card?

Login at account.mypaymentvault.com to check your balance and manage your card account.

My card was declined even though I have sufficient funds available for my transaction - what should I do?

If your card is declined at point-of-sale, please ask the merchant to run your card as 'credit'. Your card may be declined if it is run as a debit card, even if you have funds available. If you continue to experience issues, please call +1 (888) 633-1275.

Once I receive my Mastercard Prepaid Card, can I still choose to redeem my points in other ways?

Yes! The KOHLER Rewards Prepaid Mastercard is an additional rewards redemption option available to you. You can choose to redeem Kohler Preferred Partner points at any time on the Kohler Preferred Partner Market or use your points to load your KOHLER Rewards Prepaid Mastercard or do both! It's entirely up to you!
